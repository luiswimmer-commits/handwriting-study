Handwriting samples for a study

Version 0.1. Please read this before you start.

Who we are

Luis Felipe Breves Wimmer, Calgary, Alberta, Canada, makes a note-taking app that reads handwriting. Contact: support@lemmascribble.com.

What you do

You copy formulas or sentences that the page shows, with your stylus. Copy only what is shown. Do not write your name or anything else about you or another person.

What we store

For each item you write:

  • your handwriting: where the pen moved and when, and the size of the writing area and of its lines;
  • the formula or sentence you copied, how large it was shown, its language, and your study group;
  • the kind of pointer that made each stroke (the page takes only a stylus);
  • your screen's size and your browser's name;
  • your Prolific ID and the study's and session's IDs from Prolific;
  • the version of this agreement and the time it was stored.

We do not store your name, e-mail, location or any other part of your device. The page uses no cookies, no advertising and no analytics. While you write, the page keeps your progress in this browser tab only, so that a reload can carry on; it is deleted when you finish or close the tab.

What it is for

We use your samples to build, train, test and improve handwriting recognition. These models are used in a commercial app and may be shipped inside it. We keep your samples for as long as they are useful for this, or until you ask us to remove them.

Your permission

You keep ownership of your handwriting. By pressing "I agree" you give Luis Felipe Breves Wimmer permission, worldwide, without an end date and with no payment beyond the study's own payment on Prolific, to store, copy and use your samples for the purpose above.

Your Prolific ID

Your samples are kept without your name. We use your Prolific ID only to pay you through Prolific and to find your samples if you ask us to remove them.

Where it is stored

On the servers of our hosting provider, Supabase, in Canada. By pressing "I agree" you agree to this transfer to Canada. The hosts of this page and of the storage may keep ordinary technical logs, such as your internet address, under their own terms; we do not add them to your samples.

Stopping and removal

You may stop at any time by closing the page and returning the study on Prolific; you are then not paid. Items you wrote before you stopped are already stored.

To remove your samples, write to support@lemmascribble.com with your Prolific ID, at any time. We will delete them and leave them out of all training from then on. A model that was already trained with them is not changed.

You may also ask to see your samples, and you may complain to the data protection authority where you live.

You confirm

  • You are 18 or older.
  • The handwriting you give is your own.
